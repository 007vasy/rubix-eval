How to turn the cube
  • L Click a sticker — clockwise 90°
  • R Right-click a sticker — the other way
  • 2× Double-click a sticker — 180°
  • drag Drag empty space — look around
  • ✓ Green button — submit when solved
  • Load = official puzzle for that size and turns. Shuffle = a new random scramble.
